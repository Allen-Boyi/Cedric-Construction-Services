Tiling
Tiling · Best for wet & high-traffic zones

Porcelain Tiles

The ultimate choice for high-traffic and wet zones. These tiles are incredibly dense and offer superior water resistance, making them perfect for kitchens, bathrooms, and even outdoor patios. Their near-zero porosity means they resist staining and frost, giving you decades of performance with minimal maintenance.

💧 Water Resistant 🏗️ High-Traffic 🌿 Outdoor Safe ⚡ Low Maintenance
Tiling
Tiling · Ideal for indoor living areas

Ceramic Tiles

A versatile and popular indoor flooring option. They offer a great balance of style and durability, performing best in living areas where a wide range of colours and finishes is desired. Ceramic tiles are lighter and easier to cut than porcelain, making them a cost-effective choice for large surface areas.

🎨 Wide Range of Finishes 🏠 Indoor Specialist 💰 Cost Effective
Tiling
Tiling · Luxury marble, granite & slate

Natural Stone

Bring luxury and a unique aesthetic to your space with marble, granite, or slate. No two slabs are ever identical, giving each installation a one-of-a-kind character. While durable and timelessly beautiful, these require professional sealing to maintain their appearance in high-end living areas and feature walls.

💎 Luxury Finish 🪨 Unique Every Time 🔒 Requires Sealing 🏆 High-End Appeal

Flooring Options

3 Materials
Flooring
Flooring · Modern high-traffic indoor solution

Vitrified Tiles

Engineered for strength and low porosity, vitrified tiles are a modern solution for high-traffic indoor environments. They offer a polished, seamless look that is highly resistant to stains, scratches, and chemicals. Ideal for large open-plan spaces where a clean, refined aesthetic is the goal.

🛡️ Stain Resistant ✨ Polished Finish 🏢 Large Format ⚙️ Low Porosity
Flooring
Flooring · Budget-friendly & moisture resistant

Vinyl Flooring

A practical, budget-friendly, and moisture-resistant option. It's particularly popular for kitchen upgrades and renovations where comfort underfoot and easy installation are priorities. Modern luxury vinyl planks convincingly replicate the look of hardwood or stone at a fraction of the cost.

💰 Budget Friendly 💧 Moisture Resistant 🦵 Comfort Underfoot 🔧 Easy Installation
Flooring
Flooring · Rugged, slip-resistant & outdoor ready

Quarry Tiles

Known for their rugged, earthy charm and slip-resistant surface. These unglazed tiles are exceptionally tough, making them a go-to for mudrooms, utility areas, and outdoor walkways. Their natural texture improves grip underfoot and their dense clay body makes them nearly impervious to the elements.

🧱 Unglazed Clay 🌧️ Outdoor Ready 👟 Slip Resistant 💪 Extremely Tough
