Company Profile

Cedric Construction Services is a trusted construction and interior finishing company providing expert tiling, fixtures, fittings, and renovation services across Cape Town and surrounding areas. We specialize in delivering durable, modern, and high-quality finishes tailored to each client's unique style and needs.

With years of hands-on experience and many completed projects, we have built a reputation for transforming homes and commercial spaces with precision and professionalism. Our team of skilled craftsmen takes pride in every project, ensuring attention to detail and superior workmanship.

Based in Wallacedene, Kraaifontein, we serve clients throughout Cape Town, bringing our expertise in tiling, fixtures installation, custom fittings, and complete renovation solutions to residential and commercial properties alike.

What sets Cedric Construction Services apart is our comprehensive approach to property transformation. We understand that a renovation is more than just a series of tasks — it's the realization of a vision. By integrating core structural work with fine interior finishing, we eliminate the need for multiple contractors, providing our clients with a streamlined, stress-free experience from the first brick to the final coat of paint.

Proudly Local — Cape Town

As a locally owned business, we understand the specific architectural styles and environmental challenges of the Western Cape. From the coastal moisture considerations in our painting and roofing techniques to the modern tiling trends in Cape Town's suburbs, our work is built to withstand the local climate while enhancing your property's value.
