Interior Specialist

Interior Painting
& Plastering

Transform your living and working spaces from the inside out. We combine skilled brushwork with expert plastering to create interiors that are smooth, vibrant, and built to last. Every room gets our full attention from prep through to the final coat.

  • Walls & Ceilings
    Full-room preparation, priming, and finishing in any colour or sheen — from matte wash to high-gloss feature walls.
  • Trim & Cabinetry
    Precision-cut painting of skirting boards, cornices, door frames, and kitchen cabinetry for a crisp, professional finish.
  • Plaster Repair & Skimming
    From hairline cracks to full re-skim, we restore damaged or uneven plaster surfaces to a glass-smooth standard before painting.
  • Specialty Finishes
    Venetian plaster, limewash, textured coatings, and decorative effects to create distinctive, design-led spaces.
Exterior
Exterior & Protection

Exterior Painting
& Protection

Your home's exterior is its first line of defence against the elements. We apply premium weather-resistant coatings using thorough surface preparation techniques that extend the life of your paintwork and protect the structure beneath.

  • Siding & Stucco
    Full exterior repaint of all cladding types — face brick, plaster, fibre cement, and stucco — using UV-stable elastomeric coatings.
  • Wood Restoration
    Sanding, sealing, and re-coating of timber fascias, window frames, pergolas, and decking to halt rot and restore natural beauty.
  • Surface Prep
    Professional pressure washing, crack filling, bonding agent application, and primer coating — the foundation of a long-lasting exterior finish.
  • Masonry Coatings
    Damp-proof and waterproof masonry treatments for boundary walls, retaining walls, and foundations exposed to moisture and weathering.
