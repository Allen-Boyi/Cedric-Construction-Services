Essential Roof Installation

A complete, professionally managed roof replacement. We handle every phase from structural assessment to final waterproofing, delivering a system engineered for the specific weather conditions and architectural requirements of your property.

  • Full System Replacement
    Complete removal of old roofing and installation of a new, fully warranted system from decking to ridge cap.
  • Deck Health Check
    Full inspection and repair of the underlying roof deck to ensure a solid, rot-free foundation before any new material is laid.
  • Critical Waterproofing
    Professional-grade underlayment and flashing installation at every penetration, valley, and eave to create a watertight barrier.
  • High-Quality Finishing
    Premium IBR sheeting, tiles, or thatch — whatever suits your home — installed with precision for lasting kerb appeal.
Repairs & Maintenance
Service B

Targeted Repair & Maintenance

Don't replace when you can restore. Our repair team diagnoses the root cause of every leak or failure and delivers a lasting fix — not a patch job. Regular maintenance contracts are available to keep your roof performing year after year.

  • Precision Leak Repair
    Advanced diagnostics to find the true source of the leak — not just the symptom — and seal it permanently.
  • Storm Damage Restoration
    Rapid assessment and repair of damage caused by high winds, hail, or fallen debris — with insurance report assistance available.
  • Flashings & Seals
    Re-sealing and replacement of failed metal flashings around chimneys, skylights, and vents — the most common source of leaks.
  • Roof Tune-Up Service
    Annual or bi-annual inspection, gutter clearance, and minor repairs to keep your roof in peak condition and prevent costly failures.
  • Emergency Tarping
    24-hour emergency tarping service to protect your interior from water damage while permanent repairs are being arranged.
