General Renovations
Modernising Within

Reimagine your existing space with structural precision. We specialise in modernising interiors while preserving the character and integrity of your home, from knocking through walls to full property refurbishments.

  • Structural Remodelling — Steel Beam Installation
    Safe removal of load-bearing walls and professional steel beam installation to create open-plan living spaces.
  • Complete Property Refurbishment
    Full-scope modernisation — electrics, plumbing, finishes, and structure — managed as a single coordinated project.
  • Period Property Restoration
    Sensitive restoration of heritage features — cornice work, sash windows, brick pointing — respecting the original character.
  • Basement & Attic Finishing
    Convert raw basement or attic space into habitable, insulated, and fully finished rooms with proper waterproofing.
